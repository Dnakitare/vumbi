Vumbi car models — Designersoup "Retro Rally" pack (FBX, textured rally
liveries), loaded via three.js FBXLoader. Each keeps its baked livery;
materials forced opaque and yaw-corrected to face +Z in-game.
  DeltaEvo    (Simba) — Group A hatch
  BoxerRX     (Nyoka) — AWD turbo
  RSTurbo     (Chui)  — RWD turbo
  K10City     (Swala) — kei turbo

Provenance: the pack shipped as "Designersoups Retro Rally Pack.zip" with no
license or readme inside; FBX metadata shows the working name "Pixel Car Art
Pack". Only the textures the models actually reference are included here
(rally skin, tread, wheel per car); street/plain skin variants, dirt maps, and
source files from the pack are not redistributed. These models are the pack
author's work, not covered by this repository's MIT license. If you are the
author and want different attribution or removal, open an issue.
