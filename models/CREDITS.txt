Vumbi car models — "Retro Rally Pack" by designersoup, purchased on itch.io
(https://designersoup.itch.io/retro-rally-pixel-art-asset-pack). Loaded via
three.js FBXLoader; each keeps its baked livery, materials forced opaque and
yaw-corrected to face +Z in-game.
  DeltaEvo    (Simba) — Group A hatch
  BoxerRX     (Nyoka) — AWD turbo
  RSTurbo     (Chui)  — RWD turbo
  K10City     (Swala) — kei turbo

The store page states no explicit license, so the raw model files are NOT
included in this repository: they ship only inside the deployed game, which is
ordinary use of a purchased pack. To run Vumbi from source, buy the pack ($9)
and unzip the four car folders into public/models/rally/ (DeltaEvo/, BoxerRX/,
RSTurbo/, K10City/ — each with its .fbx and .fbm texture folder). These models
remain designersoup's work and are not covered by this repository's MIT
license.
